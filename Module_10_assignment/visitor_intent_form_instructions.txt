WHAT TO DO

Each question shows the first message a visitor typed on a real estate team’s website chat. The agent had already opened the chat. You do not see anything else.

Some words are hidden. They do not change the category.

- (name) is a person on the real estate team.
- (company head figure) is the lead person for that company.
- (company name) is the real estate company.
- (address) is a street address.
- [email] and [phone] are contact details.

Ignore typos.

Read only that message. Choose one answer:

buyer
seller
renter
media outreach
realtor network
other

Use the list below in order. Stop at the first category that fits. Do not pick a later category because it also seems possible.


1. SELLER

Choose seller if they want to sell or list a home they already own.

Choose seller even when the same message also describes the next home they hope to buy.

Do not choose seller just because they want a home that is already on the market. That person is trying to buy.

Example: “Please list the house we already own.”


2. RENTER

Choose renter if they want to lease a home to live in.

This includes leasing for a while and buying at a later time.

Do not choose renter when they want to buy a home and then let guests stay there, or when they ask what a home has earned from guests.

Example: “We need a one-year lease for a place to live.”


3. BUYER

Choose buyer if they want to buy, or they are shopping for a home to own.

This includes a house, condo, townhome, new construction, a tour, listings, a budget, pre-approval, a second home, or a home they would purchase and then let guests use.

Also choose buyer when they do not say “buy,” but they are looking for a home, looking at houses, moving to an area, or describing the home they want, and they never say they want a lease.

Seeing the team on video does not change this. If they also state a housing plan, use seller, renter, or buyer.

Example: “We hope to purchase and would like a few options to consider.”


4. REALTOR NETWORK

Choose realtor network if they are not asking for their own home, and any of these is true:

- They say they are an agent, a broker, or with a brokerage.
- They say they have a client, and they want that client referred or helped.
- They ask about a job or about joining this office.
- The whole message asks whether this chat belongs to a certain team or brokerage.

A friend, coworker, or colleague is not a client. Saying that someone suggested this chat does not make the visitor a realtor.

Example: “I work for a brokerage in another state and I am sending a client your way.”


5. MEDIA OUTREACH

Choose media outreach if they ask for a person’s email, ask to speak with a person, or ask for a comment, quote, or interview, and they do not ask to buy, sell, or rent.

Passing that contact to a friend is still media outreach when the visitor does not ask the team to represent anyone.

Do not use media outreach when they say they are an agent or that they have a client. That is realtor network.

Seeing the team on video, by itself, is not media outreach.

Example: “I am producing a segment and need a short quote from the team.”


6. OTHER

Choose other when none of the above fits.

Use other for a greeting only, a yes or no with no housing plan, a question that only asks who the agent is, a follow-up that states no housing plan, a neighborhood or environment question, or a question that only asks whether the office manages homes for owners.

Mentioning a home they own does not make it seller unless they ask to sell that home.

Example: “Good afternoon.”


QUICK CHECKS

- They want to sell a home they own, even if they also describe the next purchase: seller.
- They want to purchase a home that is on the market, including one guests would use: buyer.
- They want a lease to live in, including a lease now and a purchase later: renter.
- They ask what a home they might buy has earned from guests: buyer.
- They plan to relocate and never mention a lease: buyer.
- They are an agent, they have a client, they ask about joining the office, or they only ask if this is a certain team: realtor network.
- They ask for a person’s contact, a conversation with that person, or a quote, and they state no housing plan: media outreach.
- They only greet, ask who the agent is, ask about the area, or ask whether the office manages homes: other.
