WHAT WE NEED YOU TO DO

You will see 30 short chat messages, labeled A1 through A30. For each message, choose exactly one label: buyer, seller, renter, or other.

Read only the words in that message. Do not guess from later conversation, and do not add an explanation. If the intent is unclear, choose other.


WHERE THESE MESSAGES COME FROM

These are the first things a visitor typed in a live chat on a Florida real estate team’s website (ReadyChat). An agent opened the chat (for example, “Sorry to interrupt…”). We kept only the visitor’s first reply. Each line is one visitor’s opening message.


HOW TO LABEL EACH MESSAGE

buyer — The visitor wants to buy, or is clearly shopping for a home or property to own. This includes houses, condos, land, investment purchases, short-term rentals they would buy, tours, listings, budgets, and pre-approval.
Example: “Hi xxxxx! My husband and I will be in town next week looking for a primary residency that we will turn into an Air BNB within the next year.”

seller — The visitor wants to sell or list a property they own.
Example: “I have two investment properties here that I would like to sell next summer.”

renter — The visitor wants to lease a place to live in as a tenant. They are not trying to buy.
Example: “I am looking to rent a house 2/2 or 3/2., fenced yard and screened in porch.”

other — The message does not clearly show an intent to buy, sell, or rent. Use this for greetings only, “who are you?”, requests for a staff email, general questions about the area, questions about property management only, or text that is too vague to tell.
Examples: “Hi there.” / “Its said poinciana fl is a bad are is this true”


WHEN A MESSAGE IS UNCLEAR

- Use only this first message.
- Buying a home to use as an Airbnb, or asking about rental income on a home they might buy, is buyer.
- Choose renter only when they clearly want to lease a place to live.
- If the message mentions both buying and selling, pick the intent that is the main point of the message.
- If you would be guessing, choose other.
